How we're different: SITREP

Before we pick up a camera, we figure out what's actually worth making. We map where your comms are landing, where they're failing, and which untold story has the highest probability of shifting perception. We call that map a Situation Report, aka SITREP.

01

Ingest

Full-spectrum intelligence

Internal data, audience intelligence, public sentiment, market signals, competitive positioning. Then wider: geopolitical factors, industry shifts, emerging narratives.

02

Synthesize

AI-driven sense-making

LLMs on the full data set. Blind spots in comms, trust gaps with audiences, narrative opportunities competitors missed.

03

Execute

Documentary-grade production

Real environments, real people, observational production. Distribution plan built alongside production.
