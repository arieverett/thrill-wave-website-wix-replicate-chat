The Importance of and Data Behind Advertising on Social Media

Chris Kuzman · May 19, 2023 · Thrill Wave

Paid social gives businesses precise distribution, fast feedback and the ability to test multiple messages. Those advantages only matter when the creative gives the audience a reason to stop and care.

Target with a hypothesis

Use audience targeting to test a clear assumption about who needs the message. Avoid treating detailed targeting options as a substitute for understanding the customer.

Make the first seconds count

People scroll quickly. Lead with a strong visual, recognizable problem or specific claim, then earn the rest of the viewer's attention.

Test creative, not just audiences

Different hooks, edits, offers and proof points can produce dramatically different performance with the same targeting. Build testing into the production plan.

Read performance in context

Cost per click or view is not the final goal for most businesses. Connect platform metrics to the quality of traffic, leads and downstream conversion.

The practical takeaway

Good creative starts with a clear problem and a clear audience. Before choosing a format, define what needs to change, what evidence the audience needs, and where the finished work will actually be seen. That discipline makes production more useful and gives the campaign a better chance to perform.
