The Importance of Using Brand Guidelines

Chris Kuzman · May 25, 2023

Brand guidelines create consistency across teams, campaigns and platforms so a company presents a recognizable identity.

Consistency is key

Shared rules for color, type, imagery and voice make the brand easier to recognize wherever customers encounter it.

Unify the team

Guidelines reduce guesswork for internal teams and external partners by defining how the brand should look and sound.

Protect brand equity

A consistent identity builds familiarity and credibility over time.
