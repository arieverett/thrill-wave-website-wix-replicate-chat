The Importance of Using Brand Guidelines

Chris Kuzman · May 25, 2023 · Thrill Wave

Brand guidelines give internal teams and outside partners a shared system for how a company should look, sound and behave. They reduce inconsistency and make future creative work faster to produce.

Create recognizable consistency

Colors, typography, logo usage, imagery and voice should feel related across a website, campaign, presentation and social feed. Consistency makes the brand easier to recognize without requiring every asset to look identical.

Give creative teams useful boundaries

Good guidelines do not eliminate creativity. They define the elements that must stay consistent and the areas where a designer, editor or filmmaker has room to interpret the brand.

Include motion and video rules

Traditional brand books often stop at print and web. Modern guidelines should also address title cards, captions, lower thirds, animation, music, interview treatment and the tone of social video.

Keep the system current

Brand guidelines should evolve when the company, audience or channels change. Review the system periodically and document new patterns once they prove useful.

The practical takeaway

Good creative starts with a clear problem and a clear audience. Before choosing a format, define what needs to change, what evidence the audience needs, and where the finished work will actually be seen. That discipline makes production more useful and gives the campaign a better chance to perform.
