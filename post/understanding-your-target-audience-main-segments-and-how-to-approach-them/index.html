Understanding Your Target Audience: Main Segments and How to Approach Them

Chris Kuzman · May 29, 2023

Understanding who you are speaking to shapes the message, channel and creative approach of a campaign.

Segment the audience

Look at demographics, behavior, interests, needs and buying context rather than treating every potential customer the same.

Adapt the message

Different segments respond to different benefits, proof points and creative styles. Build messaging around the audience's actual priorities.

Choose channels intentionally

Reach each segment where it already spends attention, then measure response and refine the mix.
