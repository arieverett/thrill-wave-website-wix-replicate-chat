Harness the Power of Video Advertising to Grow Your Financial Services Business

Chris Kuzman · May 10, 2023 · Thrill Wave

Video advertising can help financial-services organizations combine education, credibility and targeted digital distribution in a format that is easy to consume.

Use video to simplify complexity

Explain one topic at a time and use examples, graphics or interviews to make unfamiliar services understandable.

Put credible people on camera

Subject-matter experts and satisfied clients can provide proof that feels more trustworthy than generic promotional language.

Target by need and context

Distribution should reflect who is most likely to need the service, where they are in the decision process and which concerns need to be resolved.

Track qualified outcomes

Views can show attention, but leads, appointments and qualified opportunities are more useful measures of whether the campaign is helping the business grow.

The practical takeaway

Good creative starts with a clear problem and a clear audience. Before choosing a format, define what needs to change, what evidence the audience needs, and where the finished work will actually be seen. That discipline makes production more useful and gives the campaign a better chance to perform.
