Exploring the Different Types of Video Commercials to Market Your Business

Chris Kuzman · May 30, 2023

Video commercials can serve very different marketing goals. The key is matching the format to what the audience needs to understand or feel.

Explainer videos

Use clear visuals, narration and graphics to simplify a product, service or idea.

Product demos and testimonials

Demonstrations show the offer in action, while testimonials add social proof through real customer experience.

Brand and behind-the-scenes video

Brand films create emotional context. Behind-the-scenes work can build trust by showing the people and process behind the business.

Live video

Live formats work well for launches, events and Q&A because they let audiences interact in real time.
