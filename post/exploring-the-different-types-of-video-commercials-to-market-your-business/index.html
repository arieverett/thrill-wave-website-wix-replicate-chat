Exploring the Different Types of Video Commercials to Market Your Business

Chris Kuzman · May 30, 2023 · Thrill Wave

Video commercials are not one format. A testimonial, product demonstration, brand film and short paid-social spot each solve a different communications problem. Choosing the format should come after choosing the job the video needs to do.

Explainers and product demonstrations

Explainers are useful when the audience needs clarity. Product demos add proof by showing the offer in use. Both formats benefit from tight structure: identify the problem, show how the product or service addresses it, and give the viewer a clear next step.

Testimonials and documentary stories

Real customers or stakeholders can provide evidence a brand cannot credibly give itself. Documentary-style interviews are strongest when the subject speaks naturally about a real problem, decision and outcome rather than reciting scripted praise.

Brand films and lifestyle spots

Brand films create context around values, identity and emotion. Lifestyle work shows the product or service inside the world of the customer. These formats are less about explaining every feature and more about making the audience understand what the brand feels like.

Short-form and performance creative

Paid social often needs a faster hook, vertical framing and multiple edits. The best production plan anticipates these versions on shoot day so the campaign does not rely on awkward crops of one master video after the fact.

The practical takeaway

Good creative starts with a clear problem and a clear audience. Before choosing a format, define what needs to change, what evidence the audience needs, and where the finished work will actually be seen. That discipline makes production more useful and gives the campaign a better chance to perform.
