How to Create User-Generated Content (UGC) for Your Small Business

Chris Kuzman · May 8, 2023

User-generated content can give small businesses a more direct, relatable way to show products and customer experience.

Make participation easy

Give customers a simple prompt or reason to share.

Prioritize authenticity

UGC works because it feels less polished and more personal than conventional advertising.

Repurpose thoughtfully

Strong submissions can support organic social, ads, websites and product pages with the creator's permission.
