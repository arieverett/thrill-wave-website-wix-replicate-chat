How to Create User-Generated Content (UGC) for Your Small Business

Chris Kuzman · May 8, 2023 · Thrill Wave

User-generated content can give small businesses relatable social proof. The strongest UGC programs make participation easy, protect authenticity and have a clear plan for permission and reuse.

Define the goal first

Decide whether the campaign should increase awareness, create product proof, generate leads or support paid advertising. The goal determines what kind of contribution you ask customers to make.

Give people a simple prompt

Specific requests are easier to act on than “share your story.” Ask for a clear photo, short clip, use case or before-and-after experience, and explain how the content may be used.

Curate for relevance, not polish

UGC works because it feels human. Select content that communicates the customer experience clearly and fits the brand without removing the qualities that made it authentic.

Get permission and repurpose intentionally

Make usage rights clear before turning customer content into advertising. Strong submissions can support organic social, paid media, product pages and landing pages when the creator has agreed to that use.

The practical takeaway

Good creative starts with a clear problem and a clear audience. Before choosing a format, define what needs to change, what evidence the audience needs, and where the finished work will actually be seen. That discipline makes production more useful and gives the campaign a better chance to perform.
