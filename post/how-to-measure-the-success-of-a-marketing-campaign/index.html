How to Measure the Success of a Marketing Campaign

Chris Kuzman · May 31, 2023

Marketing campaigns need more than activity metrics. This article looks at defining goals, measuring performance, and using results to improve future campaigns.

Start with clear goals

Decide what success means before launch. Brand awareness, lead generation, engagement, sales and retention require different measures.

Measure the right signals

Track reach, engagement, conversion and revenue alongside qualitative feedback. The useful metric is the one tied to the campaign objective.

Learn and adjust

Review results while the campaign is active, identify what is working, and make changes rather than waiting until the end.
