Creating a Compelling Marketing Strategy

Chris Kuzman · May 24, 2023

A useful marketing strategy connects business goals, audience insight, positioning, channels and measurement.

Define the objective

Start with a specific business outcome rather than a list of tactics.

Know the audience

Research what the customer values, what blocks action and which messages can change perception.

Build the channel plan

Choose the formats and platforms that fit the goal, then measure performance and adjust.
